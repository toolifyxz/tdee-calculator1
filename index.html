What is TDEE?

TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure. It is an estimate of how many calories your body uses each day when your resting energy needs and normal activity are taken into account.

Your TDEE can be useful as a starting point when setting calorie goals for fat loss, maintaining your current weight or supporting muscle growth.

How is BMR calculated?

This calculator uses the Mifflin–St Jeor equation, a commonly used predictive equation for estimating resting energy expenditure.

Male BMR = (10 × weight kg) + (6.25 × height cm) − (5 × age) + 5

Female BMR = (10 × weight kg) + (6.25 × height cm) − (5 × age) − 161

TDEE = BMR × Activity Multiplier

How are the macros calculated?

Once your daily calorie target is calculated, the calculator divides those calories between protein, carbohydrates and fat.

Protein and carbohydrates provide approximately 4 calories per gram, while fat provides approximately 9 calories per gram.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good TDEE?

There is no single "good" TDEE. Your TDEE depends on factors such as age, sex, body size and activity. The useful number is your own estimated maintenance calorie level.

Is TDEE the same as maintenance calories?

In this calculator, yes. TDEE represents estimated calories needed to maintain your current body weight at your selected activity level.

How accurate is a TDEE calculator?

TDEE calculators provide estimates rather than laboratory measurements. Activity level and individual differences can make your actual energy expenditure higher or lower. Use your result as a starting point and compare it with changes in your body weight over time.

What are macros?

Macros, short for macronutrients, are protein, carbohydrates and dietary fat. They provide energy and play different roles in the body.

Should I eat exactly the calories shown?

These numbers are estimates and are not medical advice. Individual calorie needs can vary. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, are under 18, or have specific dietary needs, speak with a qualified healthcare or nutrition professional.
